La propuesta y la idea central se integraron en una sola sección con imagen vertical, explicación del diseño, síntesis conceptual y enlaces. New composition: camera overhead, ONE pen aligned nearly VERTICALLY along the center of the portrait frame, with entire body and exposed nib visible, cap detached beside the upper part. Warm ivory paper under product, a small broad translucent purple highlight and a fine underline alongside the nib to illustrate two strokes, a subtle warm brown tabletop edge and a muted purple paper corner in the background. Premium real stationery photography, believable manufactured scale and contact shadows, natural soft directional light, real paper grain, no glossy CGI toy look. Keep the entire pen and cap fully inside frame. No hands, no notebook text, no prices, no feature labels, no watermark, no extra pens. The only lettering is small 'lin' on the pen.
